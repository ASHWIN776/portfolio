Insightful account on why being impatient is actually good because it strongly points to **"yearning for results"**.

Ben explains how speed helps in literally everything by quoting some popular personalities, and things from his own experiences.

I strongly believe - through speed, you end up doing lots of things in short frame of time and now have lot of information to process. Processing the information creates tacit knowledge, which helps you take better decisions in the future.

Pushing the ["action produces information"](https://x.com/brian_armstrong/status/1990073384022020290?s=20) game!
