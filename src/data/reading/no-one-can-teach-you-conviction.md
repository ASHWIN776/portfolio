Excellent article on why doing things yourself is better than shadowing superiors/high throughput people or having them give a pass on your decisions (safety net).

Taking action gives you straight answers - what went wrong, what was the right step. All of these contributing to your tacit knowledge. On the other hand, shadowing or acting as a bystander - we see the whole thing play out from the stands - which doesn't give you 100% answers which is highly misleading. What you want to take good decisions is to have conviction which comes from tacit knowledge which inturn comes from taking action yourself.

Ben talks about how the dynamic changed when he was working under some notable seniors to when he was the person calling the shots. Very insightful read.
